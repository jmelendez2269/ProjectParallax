Thesis

Turrell treats light as material, not illumination. Color is felt, not labeled. Edges dissolve. Time slows. The viewer becomes part of the work.

Translating this into a software brand means: fields not icons, gradients that breathe, afterimages, and a palette pulled from Skyspaces & Ganzfelds rather than from a color wheel.

01Color philosophy

Five Turrell ideas we'll borrow. Each maps to a behavior the brand can adopt.

Skyspaces

An aperture frames sky & engineered light. The frame teaches the eye to see new colors in the natural one.

→ Logo apertures · framed glow

Ganzfeld

Total color field, no edges. Eye loses focus. Brain re-builds the image from a wash of pure pigment.

→ Backgrounds · loading screens

Roden Crater

An astronomical instrument for the eye. Color tracks celestial time. Solstices become palette events.

→ Time-aware UI · Chronos identity

Retinal afterimage

Stare at red, look away — see green. Turrell uses this; we'll use complementaries to define hover & focus colors.

→ Hover states · focus rings

Breathing time

Color shifts over minutes, not frames. UI brand surfaces drift slowly — never a static brand color.

→ Background drift · brand "alive"

Light as object

The glow is the thing. No outline, no surface — just luminous mass with a soft edge.

→ Logo construction · mascot
