02Sightline family — refined

Your favorite. The diagrammatic eye → near → far. Murky colors gone — these read with crisp ink-on-paper diagrammatics for the technical feel, then warm sun-yellow + cyan as the only signal colors. Six directions.

recommend
SL-01 · DIAGRAM
Eye, near star, far disc
Ink linework · amber near + cobalt far. Reads like a textbook diagram. The classic.
SL-02 · TWO VIEWS
One eye, two truths
Same observer, two viewpoints, two outcomes. Cleanest "parallax" story.
SL-03 · OPEN EYE
Almond eye + sightlines
Eye is drawn explicitly · adds humanity to the diagram.
SL-04 · LOOKING UP
Vertical sightline
Eye at top · stargazer's view. Pairs naturally with sky.
SL-05 · MONO ON BUTTER
1-color silkscreen
Embroidery / single-pass print. Solid near, hollow far.
SL-06 · NIGHT
Sightline · dark
Butter star · cyan disc. The night-mode version.

03Star Parallax family — refined

The astronomy-correct version. Two observations of the same star, six months apart. New colors: butter star + cobalt disc · or full warm pair. Six refinements.

recommend
ST-01 · STELLAR SHIFT
Same star, 6 months apart
Amber ↔ cobalt with measurement tick. Field of background stars. Most lyrical.
ST-02 · MEASURED
Parallax angle marked
Reads as scientific instrument · "∠p" annotation echoes parallax angle.
ST-03 · ARC
Three viewpoints across time
Coral → butter → cobalt. Story of motion across the sky.
ST-04 · ORBIT
Earth's two viewpoints
The full diagram · sun, orbit, two earth positions, target star. Most literal.
ST-05 · SOLID PAIR
Two stars, one lower opacity
Most icon-friendly · works at favicon size. The "near-far" simplified.
ST-06 · GHOST
Star + memory of star
Solid past · dashed present. Afterimage in mark form.
Decision question: SL-01 (Sightline) tells a more didactic story — "this is what parallax IS." ST-01 (Star) tells a more poetic story — "this is what parallax SHOWS US." Pick one as masterbrand · the other can live as a marketing illustration.

04Kairos — past the rectangle

KA-01 was a Switch in the room. Six pivots that keep the slit-of-light idea but break the rounded-rectangle silhouette so it can't be confused for a console. Same cyan/cobalt palette.

recommend
KA-A · APERTURE DISC
Vertical slit in circle
Pure Turrell — circular Skyspace with vertical light cut. Not a Switch.
KA-B · ORGANIC FIELD
Slit in soft blob
Asymmetric · cloud-like. Most distinct from console hardware.
KA-C · DIAGONAL CUT
Lightning aperture
Same cobalt square but the slit runs diagonal. Reads as energy.
KA-D · HORSESHOE
Open aperture, dot
An open ring with a single point of light at center. Concentrated.
KA-E · TRIANGLE APERTURE
Light through pyramid
Triangle is unmistakable · slit reads as moment of clarity.
KA-F · STARBURST
Cross of light
Two intersecting slits + bright center. Like a camera lens flare.

05Stelloquy — both, with roles

Warm = primary identity. Cool = listening / processing state. Same orb, different mood. One animation rule: the orb shifts between them.

The rule: Stelloquy idles warm. While the user is speaking, it cools (listening). While Kairos is computing, it cools deeper (thinking). The moment of response — back to warm. Color = mood = what the AI is doing right now.
