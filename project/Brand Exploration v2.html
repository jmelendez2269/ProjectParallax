03Project Parallax — the iconic mark

Brief: literally show parallax. A near object misaligning against a far one depending on viewpoint. Built to stick in heads. Also has to read at 12mm, in 1 color, and as outline.

recommend
PX-01 · DOUBLE VISION
Two views, one subject
Two offset circles. The brain sees ONE object in 3D — that's parallax. Iconic, simple, works at 12mm.
PX-02 · TRIPLE OFFSET
Three viewpoints
The same circle from 3 vantage points. Reads as motion, depth.
PX-03 · SIGHTLINE
Eye → near → far
Diagrammatic — like a textbook diagram of parallax. Educational, clear.
PX-04 · CHROMATIC P
P, doubled
Stereoscopic letterform — same character, three offsets. Strong wordmark seed.
PX-05 · STAR PARALLAX
Stellar shift
How astronomers measure distance to stars. Most-on-brand for "stars" theme.

11Retinal afterimage — interactive

Stare at the coral disc for ~10 seconds, then click. The disc swaps to its complement. In the brand, this is how focus rings behave: the focus color is the complement of what your eye is on. Buttons below show this as a hover state.

How it works

Stare at a saturated color, look away — the brain shows you its complement (afterimage). We use this to make focus states feel alive: the focus halo is the complement of the button color.

→
coral → aqua
→
cyan → coral
→
lime → iris

The hover invert isn't subtle — it's the point. It mimics what your retina actually does.
