ORIGIN

Built by someone who needed it.

Project Parallax was born from a simple frustration: the tools for understanding astrology were either too technical to be useful or too vague to be trustworthy. The sky was full of information. The connection was broken.

The name comes from the astronomical phenomenon — the apparent shift in a star's position when viewed from two different angles. Two perspectives. One truth.

Kairos, Chronos, and Prismarium were built to close that gap — tools that are precise, personal, and alive to the moment. Stelloquy is the voice that makes all three feel like a conversation, not a manual.
