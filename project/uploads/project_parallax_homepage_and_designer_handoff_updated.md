# Part 1: Homepage Copy + Product-Card Wireframe

## Homepage Strategy

The homepage should not try to explain the entire mythology at once. It should:

1. Establish Project Parallax as the parent universe.
2. Introduce the three products as distinct but related tools.
3. Give each product a clear entry point.
4. Preserve the mystery and beauty of the brand without overwhelming the user.

> Let the visitor enter through one doorway, not the whole cosmos.

## Section 3 — Ecosystem Explanation

# Color Usage Ratios

## Parent Brand Pages

- 60% dark neutrals
- 20% white / readable text
- 20% spectral accents

## Product Pages

- 70% dark neutral or product-specific dark surface
- 20% product primary color
- 10% glow/accent/highlight

## Key Rule

Project Parallax may use the full tri-color spectrum. Each product should primarily use its own color family.

---

# Typography Direction

The typography should feel premium, futuristic, clear, intelligent, not overly fantasy, and not overly corporate.

## Display / Headline Font Direction

Use a geometric sans-serif with strong uppercase presence.

Suggested styles:

- Space Grotesk-like
- Sora-like
- Neue Machina-like
- Eurostile-inspired
- Orbitron only if used carefully and sparingly

## Body Font Direction

Use a readable modern sans-serif.

Suggested styles:

- Inter-like
- Manrope-like
- IBM Plex Sans-like
- Geist-like

# UI Component Direction

## Buttons

### Primary Parent CTA

- Dark background

- White text
- Subtle glow on hover

Use product-specific border and glow:

- Kairos button: violet
- Prismarium button: cyan
- Chronos button: gold

## Cards

Each product card should have:

- dark translucent surface
- product-colored border
- subtle inner glow
- icon top-left or centered
- descriptor under product name
- CTA at bottom

## Backgrounds

Use layered backgrounds:

1. Base dark neutral
2. Subtle nebula gradient
3. Product-colored glow bloom
4. Light geometric/HUD linework

Avoid putting too much visual noise behind body text.

# Final Design Brief Summary

Design a dark-mode-first, mythic-tech brand system for Project Parallax and its three sibling products.

Project Parallax is the parent universe and uses a spectral color system made from violet, cyan, and gold. The product marks are distinct but unified through geometric construction, triangular logic, sacred-geometry influence, and consistent stroke weight.

Kairos is violet and should feel personal, intuitive, and rhythm-based.

Prismarium is cyan and should feel luminous, synthetic, intellectual, and refractive.

Chronos is gold and should feel structured, archival, historical, and architectural.

The visual system should preserve the cosmic/prismatic feeling of the proof-of-concept image while becoming cleaner, more modular, and more usable across web, UI, social, slides, and product interfaces.
