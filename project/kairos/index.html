The premise

Most planning tools assume every week should look the same.

They assume progress should be linear, energy should always be available, and the answer to feeling disconnected is usually just more discipline.

Kairos starts from a different premise. It helps you understand where you are, what matters now, and how to work with your goals, timing, and natural rhythms more intelligently.

This is not about forcing more out of yourself. It is about planning with more context, more self-understanding, and more respect for the reality that life moves in seasons.

How it works

Your chart and your answers go through the same engine — together.

01
Birth data → natal chart
Your birth date, time, and location produce a real natal chart: ten planetary positions, Whole Sign house placements, and a precise snapshot of the sky at the moment you were born.
02
Your year → live transits
Kairos calculates the actual planetary positions for every day of your year. Where is Saturn right now relative to your natal Venus? When does Jupiter cross your Moon?
03
Your answers → layered intent
Your goals in your own words, what you want to release, your energy cycle rhythm — they become the second load-bearing layer the engine reads alongside your chart.
04
Synthesis → your blueprint
An AI reads your chart and your answers together. The result: 52 weeks, 12 months, 4 quarters, named activation windows, named rest periods, and a year theme that is about you specifically.
