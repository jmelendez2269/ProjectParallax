Common questions

Is Kairos only for people who are deeply into astrology?

No. While Kairos is open to symbolic and cyclical frameworks, the real goal is to create a more personal and meaningful planning system. Many users approach it as a structured framework for reflection rather than a spiritual practice. It is designed to feel grounded and useful, not gimmicky.

What does "founding beta rate locked in" mean?

If you join during the founding beta at $19/month, that price stays yours for as long as you remain subscribed — even as Kairos adds features and the standard price increases. You're not locked in to a contract; you can cancel any time. But if you do stay, you keep the founding rate.

Is this a full planner replacement?

Not necessarily for everyone right away. Kairos is designed to become a planning layer that helps you understand timing, priorities, and energy more clearly. How central it becomes to your practice depends on how deeply you want to engage with it.

What does "beta" mean here?

Beta means you're getting early access to the first live version of Kairos. The core experience is real — your blueprint is generated from your actual natal chart and real ephemeris data. But the product will continue evolving based on feedback and learning from founding members.

Why is the beta paid?

Because the beta is intended to deliver real value, not just function as a free experiment. Founding beta members receive the full planning experience at a reduced rate — and their feedback directly shapes what Kairos becomes.

What's the difference between the Planner and Planner + Oracle tiers?

Both tiers include the full personalized blueprint, week pulse, journal, and life areas. The Oracle tier adds the ability to have conversations with an AI that already knows your natal chart, current transits, onboarding answers, and any journal entries you've chosen to share with it. The Oracle is the most contextually aware piece of the system — it doesn't require you to re-explain your situation.
