Build

The year opens with Saturn trine your Midheaven — structural, grounding, patient. Q1 asks you to build the invisible architecture: the habits, systems, and commitments that will hold what Q2 wants to grow. The temptation is to push toward visibility. The smarter move is to prepare the vessel.

January
Systems before signals
Saturn direct. Focus on what you want the year's structure to hold. New Moon in Capricorn on Jan 29 — ideal for formal commitments.
February
Inner clarification

March
The readying
Mercury stations direct Mar 3. Jupiter approaching your natal Moon. The ground is being prepared. Creative Projects lane begins to warm.

Push

Jupiter's conjunction to your natal Moon (Cancer) makes this the most emotionally generative quarter of 2026. What you begin here has room to grow in ways that slower seasons simply cannot offer. The creative work of Q1 now finds its audience. Relationships deepen or clarify. The risk is expansion without ground — Saturn's trine to your Midheaven provides the structural counterweight.

Jupiter conjunct natal Moon Apr 28 — the emotional peak of the quarter. New Moon in Taurus Apr 27 — begin creative projects that require material form.
