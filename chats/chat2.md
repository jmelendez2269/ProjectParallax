**The conceptual core**

- **Kairos** (καιρός): the *qualitative* moment. The right time. Ripeness. A vertical strike of opportunity. Greek: the moment when the archer's bow is fully drawn. Not measurable — *recognized*.
- **Chronos** (χρόνος): the *quantitative* time. Sequential, steady, measurable. The river. The clock. The horizon line of history.

So in your product family: Chronos = structure, calendar, the timeline of when things happen. Kairos = intelligence, the *recognition* of the right moment within that timeline.

**The complementary pairing**

| | Chronos | Kairos |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | horizontal · river · line | vertical · arrow · strike |
| Geometry | layered planes, horizons | a single point on a line |
| Movement | continuous, slow | instant, decisive |
| Light | the sunlit field | the slit through the field |
| Visual | sun on horizon, layered chambers | the perpendicular cut, the bow at full draw, the threshold crossed |

This actually unlocks the mark. **Chronos = the horizon. Kairos = the moment something crosses it.** They're literally the same scene from two angles — Chronos is the field, Kairos is the event in the field.

**What this means for marks**

- The aperture/slit was never wrong — but it should read as a **vertical strike against a horizontal field**, not as a free-floating cobalt rectangle. Kairos and Chronos should share visual DNA: same horizon, but Kairos has the perpendicular event, Chronos has the layered ground.
- This also frees the color decision: **Kairos can be cyan + a NEW second color (not cobalt)** since the cobalt was reading as "another tech-blue brand." Pink/coral/iris are all on the table. I'll mock several.
