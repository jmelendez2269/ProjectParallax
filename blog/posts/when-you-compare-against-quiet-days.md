Stuff happens every week in history. So if you look at a few days around a planet lineup and find a pile of events, that alone does not mean much. You may have just found a normal week.

You need a comparison: how busy were the same-length stretches when that lineup was *not* happening?

That is what Chronos checked next.

Same kind of ordinary timelines. Same length of days. Quiet stretches picked in the gaps between lineups, before anyone counted.

Result: the lineup days were only a little busier. About 11% denser for one pair of slow planets. About 15% for another. Small bumps across a hundred years.

Some quiet stretches were busier than some lineup stretches. A modest bump is not proof. It is not a prediction. It does not say the planets caused anything.

A lot of astrology would call 11% “confirmation” and rush to meaning. Here the honest line is simpler: the difference is small, the limits are real, and quiet days belong in the check.

Pattern is not prophecy. Association is not tested.
